Umoria: licences
================

The game: Umoria, Robert A. Koeneke's Moria (1981-86) as James E. Wilson
wrote it in C (1987-94), kept by Michael R. Cook and others,
https://github.com/dungeons-of-moria/umoria.

- The program, the help and the screens (data/) are under the GNU General
  Public Licence, version 3 or later (LICENSE). AUTHORS names the authors.
- The Amiga port's own files: amiga/amiga_start.cpp and amiga/patches are
  under the GNU GPL version 3 or later, like the game; amiga/curses (the
  console curses), tests/, package/ and tools/ are MIT. The GlowIcon art is
  The AmiCradle Team's own.
- The source the program is built from: upstream at the commit named in
  ReadMe, with this repository's amiga/patches.
